About me

Born in the USA, raised with Uruguayan roots, and living in Spain for the past ten years.

I’ve lived in several countries and I know what it takes to start over far from everything familiar. That’s why I work with people from all over the world, in whichever language lets them express themselves: English or Spanish.

Reaching out to a therapist for the first time can be difficult. My work starts right there: making the space feel warm and safe from your very first message. I see therapy as a collaboration, where we build an understanding of your story, your strengths and what you need right now.

FAQ

What people usually ask before starting

Does online therapy work as well as in person?

For most people, yes. Research shows online therapy can be just as effective as in-person therapy, and it saves you the commute: all you need is a device, a stable connection and a quiet place to talk. It isn’t right for everyone, so if you’re unsure, write to me and we’ll talk it through.

What language are sessions in?

English or Spanish, both are my native languages. Choose whichever feels more comfortable, or use both.

Do you see under-18s?

No. Atempo works only with adults aged 18 and over.

Which countries can I join from?

Wherever you are, as long as we can find a time that works for both of us.

What if I have an emergency?

Atempo is not a crisis service. If your life or someone else’s is at risk, call 112 or your local emergency number. In Spain you can also call 024, a free 24-hour suicide prevention line.
